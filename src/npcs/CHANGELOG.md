# Flan's Ultimate 2: NPC Vehicles & Soldiers 2.2.0

First release. Requires Flan's Mod Ultimate 2.2.x (including 2.2) and Custom NPCs.

* NPC versions now use the main mod's major.minor compatibility line with an independent NPC patch revision; incompatible main-mod lines are rejected

* Generated Teams bots no longer inherit random Custom NPCs titles such as Patreon; clone and leadership titles remain available
* Destroyed vehicle parts always let rounds through; removed the `Broken parts let rounds through` option

* Teams NPC vehicles respect each vehicle spawner's shared spawn delay, allowed categories, exact definitions and all-at-once or sequential deployment setting; vehicles and soldiers spread across eligible spawners and search for clear nearby spaces, waiting when none are available; excluded vehicles wait for an eligible vehicle spawner instead of appearing at infantry spawn points
* Teams rounds have a separate NPC vehicle respawn delay through `teams ai round <round> vehiclerespawn <seconds>` and the Command Center; per-vehicle overrides are available in Vehicles > Allocations > Settings and through `teams ai round <round> team <team> vehiclerespawn <type> <seconds|inherit>`, preserving the rest of the allocation
* The Teams command centre groups capture weights under Combat and vehicle respawn, crew, wreck, emplacement and player seat deployment settings under Vehicles; the Templates tab is now called Squads and its controls fit above the round buttons
* Loose infantry squad size can be set per team under Infantry > Organization or with `teams ai round <round> team <team> squadsize <number|inherit>`; existing round-wide sizes remain the default

* The Teams command centre can save full bot settings as named presets, preview team mapping and missing content, apply them to one or all rounds, and import/export JSON files on the operator's computer

* Generated Teams vehicle bots receive their full vehicle, ship, aircraft or turret preset; saved generated bots are repaired once, including grounded aircraft and moving AA guns
* `Aircraft combat role` and `Aircraft search range` in Wolff's Mod > Movement > Flight work inside and outside battles: fighters prioritize interception and air superiority before ground support, chasing an aircraft that threatens nobody only as far as they could catch it and strafing the ground instead of crossing the battle after it, while bombers prioritize ground attacks; the new `Ground attack` role is the gun-armed counterpart of `Bomber`, supporting the ground with whatever it carries and turning on aircraft only where one goes for its own side; `Auto` now reads a gun-armed helicopter as a ground attacker rather than a bomber, so gunships still support the ground first but defend their own side against enemy aircraft instead of ignoring them; aircraft follow battle patrol assignments directly and choose supplied guns or bombs for their mission; allocation roles persist through respawns and can be set in vehicle settings or with `teams ai round <round> team <team> aircraftrole <type> auto|fighter|bomber|attacker`

## Soldiers

* Teams soldiers automatically reserve and crew real Flan driveables for squad travel, keep objective orders while driving, and unload transport passengers safely near their task; Vehicles > Real vehicles and `teams ai round <round> driverealvehicles|gunrealvehicles|riderealvehicles on|off` separately control driving, gunners and passengers (all on by default); `/teams ai board` uses the same ownership and crew rules, and dismount orders prevent immediate reboarding

* `Drive Driveables` now defaults to on for soldiers; allied players requesting an occupied seat by right click or the change-seat key take priority over NPCs in real vehicles, NPC vehicle crews, deployable guns and AA guns, with NPCs moving to a free seat or getting out; default-on `Player seat deployment` also permits vehicle-spawner deployment into allied NPC seats in real vehicles and AA guns, configurable per NPC or through `teams ai round <round> playerseatdeployment on|off`

* Soldiers can deploy carried guns and man nearby deployable and AA guns inside or outside Teams rounds, using compatible carried ammunition or the same default-ammo fallback as handheld guns, with stable mounted aiming even when their look controller updates, standing at deployable guns at the same height as players and staying at the gun through brief lulls or interruptions; enabled by default through `Man deployable and AA guns` and the bot combat setting, with `teams ai round <round> emplacements off` to disable it for a round

* Bot settings > Infantry > Class limits offers independent per-class `ClassLimit` and `ClassLimitPercent` overrides for automatically assigned infantry, preserving explicit squads, transport infantry, commanders, crews and player limits; pending respawns reserve slots, and fully capped forces wait for capacity

* `Blood Effects` in Wolff's Mod > Effects enables airborne wound puffs, severe-injury spray and temporary ground splatters; blocked hits do not bleed and fatal heavy hits do not duplicate splatters; the Player and Human NPC presets and generated soldier bots enable it, and the Teams rule can disable it for everyone

* Custom NPCs use Flan's guns, grenades, throwables, melee weapons, shields and armour, with reloads, ammunition from their inventory and Flan's sounds and muzzle flashes; the sounds an NPC makes carry as far, and as loudly, as the same weapon in a player's hands
* Soldier class loadouts from Flan's teams and classes, with skins, themed name lists and naming by class and gender; NPCs whose random Custom NPCs preset skin is missing wear Steve instead of the missing texture
* Flan NPC tactics for soldiers, vehicles and aircraft, including cover, peeking and Custom NPCs' own tactics with Flan's weapons
* NPCs throw what they carry by per-kind strategies, reserve smoke for pinned or badly wounded soldiers, avoid wasting flashbangs and gas on vehicles or incendiaries on burning targets, plant suitable mines and remote charges with a clear withdrawal route, detonate only their own armed charges around visible enemies with allies clear, and deploy supply bags for their own needs, use them immediately and reuse useful friendly bags within range for healing, effects and finite ammunition; flash and gas reach count for friendly-fire safety, and carried parachutes open when they fall
* Soldiers occasionally crouch or crawl briefly at firing positions and when approaching nearby cover, with slower movement, matching collision dimensions and no sprinting; `Tactical crouching and crawling` controls automatic posture changes, which respect authored animations and stop for close enemies or urgent movement; crawling uses the gun's prone accuracy
* NPCs drawn with the player model are hit limb by limb by Flan rounds like players: headshots and the other hit zones use the same damage modifiers, worn armour protects the part it covers, and rounds passing between the limbs miss. `Limb hitboxes` (Combat > Damage) turns this off for a single NPC
* NPCs always have their full-size hitbox: Custom NPCs left NPCs created on the server, such as Teams bots, a box one block high there, and NPCs that got off a vehicle a box 77% as high, so rounds, arrows and melee aimed at the chest or head passed over them
* Presets in the Wolff's Mod tab, such as Mob-like and Player-like, with undo; presets preserve the existing `Return to start` setting
* NPCs look for and keep targets out to their aggro range instead of only Custom NPCs' `NpcNavRange`, so planes and helicopters flying high above their targets spot and hold them; aircraft models fight no faster than lets them come round within their aggro range, so fast planes no longer fly off out of the simulated area; planes fly straight on to gain room when their target is too close beside them to turn onto, rather than circling it, and keep their target while they come round; planes and helicopters that see their goal head straight for it instead of searching a path through the air, and `Efficient path searches` covers flying NPCs too, so a single bomber no longer stalls the server
* Wandering range `0` allows unlimited roaming without triggering `Return to start` for distance; returning after combat and on respawn is preserved
* `Teleport home when inactive` (Advanced > Activity): NPCs with `Return to start` left away from home outside the simulation distance, or resting beyond their dormant range, are sent straight back once no player is near enough to see them go
* NPCs that fight back can no longer be picked off from beyond their aggro range: an enemy that hits them or fires close past them stays their target, however far off, for `Remember attackers` seconds after its last shot (15 by default), and one with nobody else to fight turns on a shooter firing past it. `Call out attackers` lets NPCs of friendly factions within that radius take the enemy on too; one that cannot reach its own target turns on it when it can (Tactics > Return fire)
* Soldiers and Teams bots take cover under fire, hide to reload, withdraw from known ineffective vehicle engagements, evade approaching hulls, spread between allies and use carried anti-tank weapons from better angles; equipped soldiers with the default Rush variant keep pursuing orders during combat, choose cover toward their destination, sprint short bounds and can fire while moving with the same weapon sprint-spread penalties as players, pausing to cover nearby advancing allies; sprinting applies the full movement-speed boost, bots sprint along objective routes without needing an enemy, and flanking soldiers sprint into position; pinned soldiers can escape into cover, and Balanced, Cautious and Aggressive profiles adjust bound distances and firing pauses (Tactics > Survival, Advancing and Movement limits)
* NPCs whose `Door interact` is set to Open, including the Player-like and Human presets, prefer cover inside buildings, fight from their windows and doorways, and follow an enemy that breaks contact by slipping into a building through its door where it was last seen, bringing idle friends nearby along and looking round the room on arrival, searching for up to 20 seconds
* `Firing range from weapon` (Combat > Detection & ranges): an NPC fires, and notices enemies, as far as the spread of the weapon it holds still lets it hit, within a limit for its kind of weapon, so a scoped rifle outranges a sub-machine gun; its own worse accuracy shortens the range
* Human limits for any NPC (Aiming > Human limits): reaction time, aim settling time, first-shot aim error, lead share, damage to players and `Never kill players`, filled in from the Teams bot difficulty levels by `Skill level` or set by hand; they put no limit on an NPC by default, and a Teams bot fights with its own again once it leaves its battle
* `Suppressed by near misses` (Tactics > Return fire): enemy rounds passing close to an NPC on foot make it aim worse, and once pinned down it moves no closer to its target until the fire lets up
* Duties for any NPC on foot (Tactics > Duties) and Teams class roles include `assault` for closer combat and objective advances, `melee` for hand-to-hand attacks after an optional opening throw, and `skirmisher` for keeping ranged distance with bows or javelins; support and MG soldiers preferentially cover advances with controlled automatic bursts and wait for allies to clear their firing line; medics heal, engineers repair, support supplies ammunition and anti-tank soldiers prefer armour; `Fall back below health` sends badly hurt soldiers to a medic or home
* `Name tag for allies only` (Effects): only players the NPC's faction is friendly to see its name tag

## Vehicles, Aircraft & Mechas

* NPC mecha drivers use swords at melee reach, cycle semi-automatic weapons and complete bursts, jump before getting stuck, use rocket packs for higher routes and mine effective obstacles under server breaking rules; mecha-model NPCs display both arm tools, select swords by reach and damage, inherit carried fall-protection, repair and lighting addons, apply protected landing damage and use authored stomp timing

* Human limits now reach every weapon an NPC fires from a vehicle. Reaction time, aim settling, first-shot aim error and `Lead share` applied only to handheld weapons and a vehicle NPC's main weapon group; its second group, its crewed and unmanned seat guns and the weapons of a real Flan driveable an NPC drives all fired instantly and perfectly whatever the NPC's skill level. A driveable lays its weapons as well as the NPC at its wheel can aim them, and rounds it fires at a player now answer to that NPC's `Player damage` and `Never kill players`
* `Unsteady on the move` (Tactics > Vehicle tactics, on by default): the motion of a vehicle's hull throws its guns off, so rounds fired while it moves and turns scatter, the more the faster it runs and the harder it turns, up to `Spread on the move`. Without a `Gun stabiliser` it also stands still for a moment to shoot, then drives on whether or not its target is down, so a standing shot never costs it its goal
* A vehicle that has shot itself dry breaks contact instead of driving on at the enemy, under `Breaks contact with unwinnable fights`; only one that really uses its ammunition up can run out
* Vehicle wheels and tracks throw up dust in the colour of the ground they run on, a hovering rotorcraft blows a ring of dust, or of ripples over water, out from under itself, and ground vehicles smoke from their exhaust while under power
* NPC vehicles use their posed solid part hitboxes for movement collisions and ground contact, including rotated, scaled and damaged hulls

* Ground vehicle NPCs pitch and roll about the vehicle origin with the terrain beneath their wheels using their model's suspension settings, probe ahead while moving and settle their model height when using mob collision; partial wheel support eases unsupported angles level, and turret and seat guns, muzzles, crew seats and part hitboxes follow the terrain pose smoothly

* Model weapons emit firing smoke, world casings and layered cannon blasts at their barrels; vehicle-shaped NPCs share water spray, machinery exhaust and destruction columns, and retained wreck effects burn out within one minute without replaying on respawn or model changes

* Flan model NPCs, including AA guns, scale projectile and explosion pushes by vehicle mass by default, using the same mass fallbacks and knockback settings as real vehicles; the per-NPC `Immune` option also blocks direct explosion pushes, while `Normal` keeps Custom NPCs behaviour

* Ship bots use the nearest clear water surface within 64 blocks of their selected spawn point, and wait to spawn when no water is available in loaded chunks
* Naval squads support the battle ashore instead of only patrolling: a squad within gun range of the objective in play holds station off it and shells it rather than steaming back to its patrol point, and a battered squad leaves the line for the patrol point farthest from the enemy. Ships of a squad take station in line astern, spaced by hull length and moved onto water deep enough to carry them, so hulls no longer pile onto one spot or wait out the round on a station left over dry land; generated ship bots also get a naval combat tactic instead of Custom NPCs' default

* NPCs drawn as Flan's vehicles, planes, helicopters, ships, mechas and AA guns move, fight, sound and take damage like their driveables, with part hitboxes, armour and HEAT overrides; armour impacts spark when blocked and show flashes, metal fragments and smoke when large shells penetrate; destroyed planes and helicopters fall under gravity; vehicle muzzle flashes replace the fallback flame particle while preserving authored shooting particles; vehicle and plane main barrels recoil only when firing shells, so machine guns no longer cycle the main gun
* NPCs board, drive and fly the Flan's driveables players place, lead their targets, land planes and watch their fuel; planes hold their dive through a strafing run on a ground target instead of levelling off short of it, so their guns bear and fire, and helicopters fighting an aircraft climb to its altitude and bring their nose further up, instead of hovering at their `Hover height` with guns that could never reach it
* NPCs driving ground vehicles and mechas drive around walls and narrow ledges where a way round exists instead of climbing over them
* Vehicle NPCs carry crews in their seats; seat guns use normal magazine reload delays and sounds, crewed seat guns pick their own targets, and crew can get out to fight or jump by parachute; when destroyed, including Teams bot aircraft, vehicles set to let crew bail out eject them with parachutes they carry
* Seated crew keep their body aligned with their seat through rendering and turns across 180 degrees; gunners turn with their gun as players do and, on vehicle NPCs, move round the gun's pivot; NPCs riding normal aircraft inherit pitch and roll like players
* Friendly players can right-click any empty seat at its position to board vehicle NPCs, including seats outside the central hitbox, occupy empty driver seats and man gunner seats, and change seats with Flan's change-seat key; the NPC still drives when a player occupies its driver seat
* NPC vehicles and drivers choose useful weapon groups and compatible ammunition for each bank, so crew MG ammunition cannot replace the main cannon's shells; MGs avoid armour they cannot penetrate, including turretless hulls and shielded crew inside NPC vehicles; after three blocked frontal shell hits they try to reach a side within their movement limits; generated Teams bots use Wandering instead of Standing so their combat paths can move them, including bots already saved in a battle; independent MG gunners choose other suitable enemies such as infantry, including outside Teams battles; seat gunners can defend without a main vehicle target; vehicles maintain engagement spacing, try lateral firing positions and can be repaired
* With infinite ammunition, NPCs fall back on every round their weapon or their model's weapon bank is issued with by default, instead of only the first of them, and choose between those and the ammunition they carry to suit the target: a piercing shell against protected hulls, a high-explosive one against infantry and unarmoured vehicles, and no more penetration than the plate in front of them needs. A round once chosen stands while a loader would still be busy with it, and ammunition the NPC really carries keeps its place ahead of a default round. `Fall back on default ammo` (Combat > Ammunition) holds an NPC to the rounds it was given instead, and the page lists the default rounds of each of its weapons
* `Rounds stocked per slot` (Combat > Ammunition) sets how many rounds of ordnance whose item stacks only one high, such as a tank shell, go into an inventory slot when an NPC is stocked with ammunition, so that a generated vehicle bot which uses its ammunition up carries more than one shell per gun
* Vehicles, aircraft and ships keep clear of their own blast: they hold their fire at a target close enough for their explosive rounds to catch them and pull back out of reach first (`Keep clear of own blast`, `Blast safety margin`)
* Ships run in bow on to fire torpedoes and anything else fixed to their hull, turn away after each run, and circle their target so the turrets along their side bear once they have none left (`Ships turn to bear`); the bow is laid where a slow round such as a torpedo meets a moving target rather than on its wake, and a course that would take the hull onto a coast or into shallows is swung off until there is water to steam through, so a ship fighting along a shoreline turns away from it instead of running aground
* Ships are steered as hulls rather than as cars: they hold their throttle through a turn, since a rudder only bites while a ship has way on, start their turns a hull length and a half early, take their way off over a long run and put their engines astern to stop on station, and reach a station behind them by steaming their turning circle instead of reversing to it. Wide hulls also read the water under themselves at a bounded number of sample columns rather than one for every block of their width, which costs far less on long naval path searches, at the price of a hull wide enough to be sampled being able to path over a shoal narrower than its sample spacing
* NPC ships trim and heel as they flood, going down by the bow or the stern and leaning to the flooded side, as a driveable ship does; one that is not in open water is also held to the same fraction of its speed a driveable ship is held to there, instead of making full way in a river or a pond
* Submarine NPCs choose their own depth (`Submarines choose their depth`): they run at their set `Depth below water surface` on their way somewhere, come up to periscope depth to fight, and go deep again for a few seconds whenever something hits them. A submerged hull is also harder to find, noticed only well inside the range the same hull would be seen at on the surface and closer the deeper it runs, so one that dives away breaks contact instead of being followed on a bearing nothing can see
* Halted tracked vehicles driven by NPCs, and standing vehicle NPCs whose turret covers their target, turn their front armour, angled about 25 degrees, toward the nearest enemy ground vehicle while the turret keeps aiming (`Frontal armour angling`, on by default)
* Vehicle, plane and mecha NPCs fire both weapon groups of their model by default (`Fire both weapon groups`): the group beside the main attack fires its guns, shells, rockets and bombs at its own cadence, and its bombs fall only on vehicles and other large targets on the ground; only a plane whose main weapon is its bombs approaches them in a level bomb run, while one whose main weapon is its guns dives on its gun run and lets those bombs go as they line up, so a plane carrying both no longer flies level past ground targets without firing; a plane setting up a gun run holds higher above its `Attack altitude` so the whole dive stays above the height it may fire from, guns an aircraft lays by flying the airframe onto the shot are given more room than a turret, an orbiting plane turns in for its pass sooner, and an aircraft's quick guns walk their rounds across a target in bursts rather than one continuous stream; a model whose chosen group is empty, such as a bomber that only carries bombs, attacks with its other group
* Players can stand and walk on the solid hull and turret of an NPC vehicle, which carries them smoothly without running them over or pushing them off; fixed sudden turns and drops from unstable carry transforms, including on large ships, and replayed movement when server time is synchronized
* The Guard job's target lists leave out Flan's driveables and NPC models, and chosen targets leave Available Targets

## Teams Bots, Factions & Battles

* Teams gamemodes alone decide victory: Conquest, Domination and Assault ignore separate bot respawn tickets; other modes retain `tickets` only as a bot respawn budget, without restricting players or declaring an attrition winner. Removed `playertickets` and the player-ticket toggle; standalone skirmishes and wars keep their attrition rules

* Armour squads sent to take an objective now cover it from a standoff rather than driving onto a point only infantry can capture: they hold about 24 blocks beyond its radius, on the side they approached from, where their guns reach the objective and the anti-tank weapons waiting on it do not reach them
* Each aircraft of a flight keeps a height of its own over its patrol, so wingmen no longer circle through their leader
* Toggle `NPC vehicles` in the Teams command centre or with `/teams ai round <round> vehicles on|off` to despawn vehicle bots and their crews and allow them to spawn again without changing Vehicles > Allocations or infantry

* Battle behaviour profiles can inherit each NPC or clone's settings or temporarily override them with Balanced, Cautious or Aggressive movement and survival choices, without changing combat attributes or saved clone settings

* `Vehicle crews` is enabled by default; vehicle bots spawn with soldiers in their seats unless crews are explicitly disabled
* NPCs attack players on Peaceful difficulty too, which Custom NPCs otherwise forbids: Teams bots always, other NPCs while `Fight players on Peaceful` (Server > Rules) is on, as it is by default
* Bot difficulty, cycled from trainee up to ace, set for a round, skirmish or war and independent of the world's difficulty: `ace` (no human limits), `elite` (nearly full use of the equipment with a human's reaction), `veteran`, `regular` (the default), `recruit` and `trainee`. Lower levels react later to a newly seen target, need longer for their aim to settle, lead moving targets less, aim worse and notice enemies less far out, while keeping the health and rate of fire their equipment gives them; `recruit` bots deal half their damage to players and `trainee` bots a quarter. `custom` fights with values of its own for everything a level sets, chosen with `Edit` beside the difficulty in the bot settings and on the skirmish and war pages, starting from any level, or given as `difficulty custom <accuracy> <aggro> <reaction> <settle> <error> <lead> <player_damage> <non_lethal>`. A changed level or custom value reaches the bots already in the field within a second of saving the settings
* `Cinematic mode` (Server > Rules for every NPC), for filming battles: players cannot die, whatever hits them, except by falling out of the world or `/kill`; NPCs still kill each other
* Teams, skirmish and war bots follow the global `botHealth` maximum-HP override from the command centre, including bots already in the field; 0 keeps their own health
* Teams soldiers follow the round regeneration rule and heal half a heart every five seconds by default; medical healing and vehicle repairs remain independent
* Teams bots made from a class fire as far as their weapon and level let them hit, about 128 blocks with an accurate rifle and up to 256 with a scoped one, and call out whoever shoots at them to the bots within 48 blocks, so a player sniping them from afar draws their fire and their marksmen and vehicles
* Teams bot vehicle wreck chance now defaults to 100%, and the wreck keeps the paintjob of the destroyed vehicle

* AI commanders assign objectives by surviving squad strength, health, ammunition, suppression and anti-armour capability, divide hunting and scouting targets between squads, spread ready infantry across capture objectives before concentrating reinforcements, and use stable early bypasses around reported ground fighting even for squads with separate objectives; all attacking infantry enter capture zones, including at small points, and squads on their way to an objective fire on the move at distant enemies instead of stopping at every firefight, so large battles no longer stall along one front line; squad leaders regroup scattered soldiers, stage assaults behind supporting fire without stopping bypasses for distant enemy reports, assign defensive sectors and coordinate withdrawals, with alternating fireteams, route-failure reports and reinforcement requests; they investigate expiring sightings and incoming-fire reports instead of tracking unseen enemies, and infantry squads reserve cover and coordinate movement with covering fire during bounds and reloads

* Optional commander bots (`commanderbot`, per-team `commanderclass`) stay behind the front and disrupt AI strategy for configurable `commanderdisruption` seconds when killed; commanders and acting bot squad leaders show leadership titles with their normal name visibility
* Teams bots: NPC soldiers, squads and vehicles that fight Teams rounds, with player orders (`Squad orders` key, O), a commander seat, transports, class roles (a medic heals and an engineer repairs once every five seconds, and the same ally or vehicle at most every ten seconds; a single bandage carried for first aid does not make every class a medic), every class of the team fielded in proportion to its weight, grenades thrown at any enemy in safe reach, suppression and squad leading; bots approach CTF flags at ground level even on taller poles
* Manage Custom NPCs factions for battles: branches of Flan's teams, alliances, wars and peace between factions
* Players can join and leave factions; factions are invite-only by default, and operators choose who may join, the leave penalty and the rejoin cooldown
* `Attack Mobs` in Global > Factions: every NPC of the faction hunts hostile mobs on sight, slimes and ghasts included, without needing the Guard job
* Skirmishes between factions outside Teams rounds, and endless wars over chunk territory
* Teams bot settings (`/teams ai editor`): a team's clone, class weights, squads, vehicles, class roles and vehicle crew classes are chosen in pickers with search, pages and previews of the clone, class loadout or squad leader; crew classes have a submenu with shared defaults, separate defaults for vehicles, planes, mechas and AA guns, and optional per-definition overrides for each seat role; squad template members use the same class browser with soldier counts and an explicit leader choice, keeping the template's ID and name while choosing; template name lists have navigation arrows and default to inheriting the team's list; vehicles are browsed by category with an optional content-pack filter, and their previews fit the full model within their panel; vehicle allocations have quantity controls, named spawn and carried-squad settings, and all paintjobs allowed by default or a selected subset, preserved through text import and export; vehicle lists fall back to definition names or shortnames and previews use their proper model textures; the count mode is explained beside each team's count, with the number of soldiers it fields, and every setting explains itself in its tooltip; Match groups bot population, respawns, objectives and display, while Combat groups fighting, squad coordination and vehicle use; Infantry groups Population, Loadout and Organization, including squads and class roles, while Vehicles groups vehicle allocations and crews; all pages fit above the round selector
* Factions, Skirmishes, Wars and Bots pages in the Teams command centre
* Configurable friendly fire between NPCs, players and allies (`wolffsmodnpcs-common.toml`, `/flansnpcfriendlyfire`, Server > Friendly fire in the Wolff's Mod tab)

## Performance

* NPCs far from every player work out their decisions every second or every fourth tick instead of every tick, which is what a large battle spends its tick time on. Their movement, physics and damage keep running every tick; equipped weapons also keep their normal firing, charge, spin-up and reload timing, checking visibility before shooting. An NPC in a seat or drawn as a vehicle, aircraft, mecha or AA gun always decides every tick. `aiTickLod`, with its distances and periods, turns this off or retunes it in `wolffsmodnpcs-common.toml`
* NPC path searches run on worker threads (`asyncPathfinding`), using separate path finders and node caches; overloaded queues defer requests, reserve room for urgent cover and escape paths and discard superseded requests, while an existing path to the requested destination can continue
* `budgetTargetMillisPerTick` works out the target and path search limits from how long the server's ticks are taking, so a battle gives up search cadence rather than tick rate and operators need not guess numbers for their hardware; 0 keeps the `SearchBudget` and `PathBudget` settings as they are set. Overdue searches share a bounded extra allowance; cover searches resume across decisions under a shared raycast budget, with additional capacity for hurt or pinned soldiers
* Target searches, call-outs to allies and duties share one list of the level's living entities per tick; nearby target searches in large populations use Minecraft's live entity sections, and searches with many rejected candidates avoid repeatedly scanning the whole candidate list
