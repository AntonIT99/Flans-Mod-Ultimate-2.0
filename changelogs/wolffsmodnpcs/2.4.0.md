# Flan's Ultimate 2: NPC Vehicles & Soldiers 2.4.0

## Compatibility

* Requires Flan's Mod Ultimate 2.4

## Soldiers and Vehicles

* NPC gunners, soldiers and crews allow for the speed-dependent drag of Flan's Mod Ultimate 2.4 rounds when they aim, so their long-range shots still land
* Aircraft and vehicles made by Custom NPCs spawners no longer vanish on their pass over a target; how far spawned creatures may roam is configurable per kind
