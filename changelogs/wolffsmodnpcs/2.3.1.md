# Flan's Ultimate 2: NPC Vehicles & Soldiers 2.3.1

## Vehicles

* NPC vehicle models turn their turrets and play their other animations again alongside ImmediatelyFast, Iris and Oculus

## Soldiers

* NPCs now walk to a supply bag already deployed nearby instead of laying out a new one for every scratch
* Soldiers mine the approaches to the ground they hold between fights, instead of only trapping enemies they can see; their mines never catch their own side while friendly fire is off (**Minefields**, on by default)

## Teams

* NPCs given a team's faction no longer shoot or retaliate against that team's round bots, or allied ones, while friendly fire is off
