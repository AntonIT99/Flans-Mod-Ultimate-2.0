# Flan's Ultimate 2: NPC Vehicles & Soldiers 2.3.0

## Teams Bots, Factions & Battles

* Skirmishes and wars can be fought under an era window with `skirmish era` and `war era`: bots swap classes and vehicles outside the period for allowed ones, and a skirmish with a team left without any refuses to start
* Melee soldiers armed with a weapon that can block, such as Jedi and Sith with lightsabers, now guard against ranged and melee enemies and lower their guard to strike
