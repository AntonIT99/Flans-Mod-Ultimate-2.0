# Flan's Ultimate 2: NPC Vehicles & Soldiers 2.3.0

## Teams Bots, Factions & Battles

* Skirmishes and wars can be fought under an era window with `skirmish era` and `war era`: bots swap classes and vehicles outside the period for allowed ones, and a skirmish with a team left without any refuses to start
* With allied spawns on in a Teams round, bot vehicles of allied teams also come in at each other's vehicle spawners
* NPC armor and melee weapons no longer wear out by default; operators can turn their durability on from the Server pages of the Wolff's Mod tab
* Melee soldiers armed with a weapon that can block, such as Jedi and Sith with lightsabers, now guard against ranged and melee enemies and lower their guard to strike

## Soldiers

* New tactics stage trench battles without a skirmish: `Charge` runs soldiers straight at the enemy without stopping for cover or pinning fire, `Assault position` sends NPCs along their Custom NPCs moving path to take and hold its last point, and `Hold line` keeps defenders fighting from cover around their post
* Operators can set or check the tactic of every NPC nearby, or of those a selector picks, with `/flansnpctactic`
* NPCs can wait in place for a start signal, the `fmu_go` entity tag, so a command block sends a whole line over the top at once
* NPCs and Teams bots load their guns as soon as they spawn or pick them up, instead of reloading when they first spot an enemy
* Wandering NPCs walk around again, and NPCs farther from players no longer stand idle instead of taking up new orders

## Vehicles

* NPC vehicles that switch weapons between targets no longer fire their main gun faster than its firing interval and reload allow
* The turrets of NPC tanks and AA guns now turn toward their targets on every client instead of staying pointed ahead, and scan their surroundings while idle, also on the move
* Seats of NPC vehicles now follow their parts as in Flan's vehicles: those in a turret die when it is destroyed, others get out of a destroyed part, which can no longer be boarded, and its seat guns fall silent
* NPC ground vehicles are slowed by sand, snow, mud and similar soft ground as player-driven vehicles are, tracked ones the least; turn it off per NPC on the Movement page or with the mod's `enableSurfaceTraction`
* NPC ground vehicles search their paths as vehicles: they keep out of gaps narrower than their hull, take no step higher than they climb, and go round steep grades and soft ground where a firm detour is quicker, and stop short of a drop instead of following a target over the edge; turn either off per NPC on the Movement page
* Engine sounds of NPC vehicles and aircraft now loop without the brief breaks between repetitions, and keep running through short pauses in movement
* NPC ground vehicles and ships no longer chase aircraft that fly faster than they drive or that their guns cannot elevate onto; they hold their ground and keep firing whenever they have a shot
* Retain wreck now also works for NPCs that respawn: they leave a copy of their body behind as they come back. Keep corpse does the same. Switching Retain wreck or Keep corpse on switches Hide killed body off, and Hide killed body switches both off. Retained wrecks are removed after the `driveableWreckLifetime` Flan's driveable wrecks last (300 seconds by default), which an NPC can override with its own Wreck time on the Effects page
* Players and NPCs riding an NPC vehicle now lean with it as it pitches and banks
* NPC aircraft drop their paratrooper crew in bigger, faster waves, up to four jumping together, over a wider area around their target
* NPC ground vehicles no longer balance on the edge of a wall or roof with half their wheels in the air: a hull with no more than half its footprint on the ground tips off, their paths go round walls and ledges that drop away behind them instead of climbing onto them, and without a path they halt short of such a wall rather than climbing it; they also watch for drops under either track, not only under the middle of the hull

## Wolff's Mod Tab

* General now shows the NPC's live status beside Start here: health, what it is doing and at whom, speed, distance from home, crew, and warnings such as reloading or out of ammo, with a bar per part and a damage top view for vehicles or the equipment of soldiers. The Flan model and Look & sound pages moved to a new Model tab
