# Flan's Ultimate 2: NPC Vehicles & Soldiers 2.3.0

## Teams Bots, Factions & Battles

* Skirmishes and wars can be fought under an era window with `skirmish era` and `war era`: bots swap classes and vehicles outside the period for allowed ones, and a skirmish with a team left without any refuses to start
* NPC armor and melee weapons no longer wear out by default; operators can turn their durability on from the Server pages of the Wolff's Mod tab
* Melee soldiers armed with a weapon that can block, such as Jedi and Sith with lightsabers, now guard against ranged and melee enemies and lower their guard to strike

## Soldiers

* New tactics stage trench battles without a skirmish: `Charge` runs soldiers straight at the enemy without stopping for cover or pinning fire, `Assault position` sends NPCs along their Custom NPCs moving path to take and hold its last point, and `Hold line` keeps defenders fighting from cover around their post
* Operators can set or check the tactic of every NPC nearby, or of those a selector picks, with `/flansnpctactic`
* NPCs can wait in place for a start signal, the `fmu_go` entity tag, so a command block sends a whole line over the top at once
