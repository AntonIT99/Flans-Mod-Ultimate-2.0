# Flan's Mod Ultimate 2.4

## Content Packs

* Content-pack item icons now get a texture atlas of their own, so many packs with large icons no longer overflow the block atlas and stop the game from loading, and large icons load faster and take less memory
* The new `Item Icon Size Limit` client option scales oversized item icons down to save video memory

## Driveables

* Pilots of tall mechas, such as the Madcat Omnimech or the Warhound Titan, and riders of other large driveables can open their inventory again from their seat, so mecha hand weapons can be equipped and fired
* Shift-clicking a gun or mecha addon into a mecha's window now equips it before filling cargo
* Items from Labjac-style packs show their `Tier` in the tooltip, and `explosionPush` lets depth charges shove a submarine harder
* Labjac-style submarines (`CanDive`) can dive and surface with V and G, hold their depth, use up oxygen while submerged and are crushed below their `MaxDepth`; depth and oxygen show beside the hotbar
* Labjac-style tanks with `FCSMode AdjustingBarrel` lase from the driver's view and raise their main gun and shells to land at the lased range, shown as FCS beside the hotbar
* Labjac-style tanks with `TurretStabilization` keep the driver's aim on target while the hull turns; press O to engage or release the stabiliser, shown as STA beside the hotbar
* Destroying the ammunition rack of a Labjac-style tank (`weakSpot` parts) kills its driver and burns the hull or turret down over the vehicle's `WeakspotCookTime`, with flames and small blasts
* Labjac-style driveables can set their death explosion radius, take reduced explosive damage, opt out of wrecks with `LeaveWreck false` and protect riders of chosen seats from all damage
* Labjac-style aircraft with a `flightCeiling` level off at it, shown beside their altitude, and driveables with a radar switch it between active and passive with F7
* Labjac-style helicopters hold their height and damp their drift when the pilot presses J (hover hold, shown beside the hotbar), and tanks with soft-kill protection (`HasPassiveAPS`) throw incoming wire- and beam-guided missiles off course
* Labjac-style driveables flagged `Death`, `bigDeath` or `nuclearDeath` go up with a far-heard blast, a fireball and a screen shake, and their wrecks last as long as their `WreckLifetime`, within the server's wreck lifetime

## Distant Horizons

* Distant planes and vehicles are drawn as pictures of their models instead of coloured boxes, inside the loaded chunks and on Distant Horizons' far terrain, where its hills hide them and its fog covers them (`Detailed Distant Driveables` option, on by default)

## Weapons

* Bullets and shells with a projectile mass now slow down with realistic, speed-dependent air drag: light rounds lose speed, damage and armour penetration at range while heavy shells keep theirs (`realisticProjectileDrag`, on by default)
* Labjac-style per-zone body armour: armour pieces rate the head, face, neck, chest, back, arms and legs separately, whatever slot they are worn in, and rounds that fail to penetrate a zone deal only a share of their damage, with a deflection sound and sparks
* Long-range ammunition from Labjac-style packs can switch to a reduced soft-target damage after a set flight time, shown in its tooltip
* Labjac-style wire-guided missiles (`HasLine`) trail a control wire that sags to the ground and fades after impact, and proportional-navigation missiles can cap their turn rate and sharpen their gain close to the target (`ProNavMaxTurnRate`, `ProNavTerminalRange`, `ProNavTerminalGain`)
* Labjac-style homing torpedoes (`modernTorpedo`) listen for the nearest driveable once they hit the water, hearing louder ones from farther off, and anti-radiation missiles home on vehicles whose radar is active
* Firing a Labjac-style TV-guided missile or bomb (`TVguided`) from a driveable seat shows its flight from the round itself until it hits
* Guided missiles now roll in flight and skim low over natural ground instead of striking it when flown level just above it
* Ammunition marked `ProjectileChunkLoading` keeps the chunks ahead of it loaded, so long-range missiles and shells are no longer lost far from players; the server limits how many fly at once

## API

* `WeaponBallistics` and `ProjectileShot` describe speed-dependent drag, and the new `BallisticDrag` helpers give aim solvers the matching drag at a target's range
