# Flan's Mod Ultimate 2.3.2

## Driveables

* Pilots of tall mechas, such as the Madcat Omnimech or the Warhound Titan, and riders of other large driveables can open their inventory again from their seat, so mecha hand weapons can be equipped and fired
* Shift-clicking a gun or mecha addon into a mecha's window now equips it before filling cargo
