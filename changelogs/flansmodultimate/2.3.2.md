# Flan's Mod Ultimate 2.3.2

## Compatibility

* Gunfire can strike and penetrate moving Sable structures, with impacts positioned on the moving hull.
* Wheeled vehicles follow moving Sable decks and can drive ashore.
* Deployable guns and AA guns can be placed and operated on moving Sable decks.
* Updated to NeoForge 21.1.267 with support for 21.1.248 and newer.
