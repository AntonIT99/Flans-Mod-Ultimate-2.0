# Flan's Mod Ultimate 2.3.1

## Performance

* The GPU model cache stays active alongside ImmediatelyFast, and alongside Iris or Oculus while no shader pack is in use
* NPC vehicle models and worn armor keep their level-of-detail savings alongside ImmediatelyFast, Iris and Oculus, also while a shader pack is active

## Teams

* Auto-balance is now off by default; enable it with the game type's `autobalance` variable
* New round rules hide player or bot nameplates from spectators and players on no team
* Mines, bags and other grenade devices placed during a round are cleared when it ends, stops or restarts
* Grenade devices placed for an NPC are no longer set off by allies its own fire spares, whatever the server allows for proximity friendly fire
* Whatever a map already carried is put back the way it was whenever a round ends, however the round used it up, destroyed, damaged or moved it: its grenade devices, vehicles and AA guns, and, on a map with bounds, the other creatures and objects standing inside them
