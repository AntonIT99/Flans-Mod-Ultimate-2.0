# Flan's Mod Ultimate 2.3.1

## Performance

* The GPU model cache stays active alongside ImmediatelyFast, and alongside Iris or Oculus while no shader pack is in use
* NPC vehicle models and worn armor keep their level-of-detail savings alongside ImmediatelyFast, Iris and Oculus

## Teams

* Auto-balance is now off by default; enable it with the game type's `autobalance` variable
* New round rules hide player or bot nameplates from spectators and players on no team
