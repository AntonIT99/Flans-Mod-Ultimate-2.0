# Flan's Mod Ultimate 2.3

## Historical eras

* Servers, dimensions and Teams rounds can limit equipment to a historical period with `/flansmodultimate era`, the `eraWindow` round rule or the command centre's Eras and Timeline pages: items dated outside it by `IssueDate` and `RetirementDate` cannot be used, obtained or boarded, are greyed out and hidden from creative and JEI, and lock the classes that carry them

## Vehicles and ships

* Ships and boats now rest steadily at their waterline instead of bobbing; downscaled legacy naval models and ships built as planes no longer sink, and the deepest-draft ships no longer slowly sink
* The ground under a vehicle's wheels now matters: ice cuts grip, braking and steering, while sand, snow, mud and similar soft ground slow vehicles down, tracked ones the least; turn it off with `enableSurfaceTraction`
* Ground vehicles with a real-world profile take steep descents slower instead of at road speed, and tilt to slopes as steep as they can climb rather than flattening out at 35 degrees
* Numbered ship turrets (`turret1` and up) stay where the pack places them instead of swinging round with the main turret as the driver looks around, along with their gunners' seats, hitboxes and shoot points; the driver's guns on them still fire where the driver aims
* Turrets placed off the vehicle's centreline turn round their own centre again, as in legacy Flan's, together with their seats, hitboxes and shoot points
* Anyone seated in a vehicle's turret, its driver included, now dies when the turret is destroyed instead of being thrown clear
* NPCs and mobs riding in a vehicle are no longer lost when its chunk unloads or the server restarts, and take their seats again when it loads
* Engine sounds of planes, vehicles and NPC aircraft now fade out gradually with distance instead of cutting out abruptly while still loud at the edge of the view distance, and no longer restart when the throttle closes for a moment

## Weapons and combat

* Melee weapons support the Labjac Edition's directional left, right and overhead swings, gun-swords, blocking and armour that deflects weak swings
* Lightsabers can now block with the aim button, showing a guard stance, and deflect blaster bolts back where you look
* Slow-firing guns such as naval and tank guns now show the wait before their next shot as a reload countdown on the HUD
* Minigun spin sounds such as the Z-6 rotary blaster's now loop without gaps, and guns can use `WarmupSound` and `CooldownSound` without a `SpinSound`; reloading mid-spin now plays the cool-down sound too
* A bullet's `KnockbackModifier` now applies to mobs and NPCs as well as players, so bullets that do not set it no longer push mobs back
* Explosion knockback now grows with the charge up to a cap: small rounds and grenades push less than before, heavy shells and bombs up to three times harder

## Armor

* Operators can choose whether Flan's armor breaks with `/armorBreaking` or the command centre's rules, and Flan's armor now wears by the damage it absorbs, as in the original mod, so it no longer breaks after a few hits nor lasts forever; worn armor can always be repaired at an anvil
* Armor sleeves now sit on the shoulders of players with slim (Alex) arms instead of sinking into the chest piece

## Teams

* In Teams rounds, armor that runs out of durability now stays on as a cosmetic without protection, so uniforms keep their look; turn this off with `/teams keepBrokenArmor false`
* Teams spectators can no longer hurt the players of a round, which they could while adventure mode was not forced, and a fighter who leaves a running round to spectate now dies like one changing sides

## Servers and performance

* Servers can now turn uncensored content on by default with `uncensoredContentByDefault`, leaving players free to turn it off, as an alternative to forcing it
* Vehicles, guns and armor now take less CPU time to draw while an Oculus or Iris shader pack is active, and under OptiFine or Fabulous graphics
* The first launch, and any launch after a content pack changes, loads faster because vehicle and gun models are now measured on several threads
