# Flan's Mod Ultimate 2.3

* Ships and boats now rest steadily at their waterline instead of bobbing, and the deepest-draft ships no longer slowly sink
* Slow-firing guns such as naval and tank guns now show the wait before their next shot as a reload countdown on the HUD
* Melee weapons support the Labjac Edition's directional left, right and overhead swings, gun-swords, blocking and armour that deflects weak swings
* Lightsabers can now block with the aim button, showing a guard stance, and deflect blaster bolts back where you look
* Servers, dimensions and Teams rounds can limit equipment to a historical period with `/flansmodultimate era`, the `eraWindow` round rule or the command centre's Eras and Timeline pages: items dated outside it by `IssueDate` and `RetirementDate` cannot be used, obtained or boarded, are greyed out and hidden from creative and JEI, and lock the classes that carry them
* Operators can choose whether Flan's armor breaks with `/armorBreaking` or the command centre's rules, and armor that fully absorbs a hit now wears out instead of lasting forever; worn armor can always be repaired at an anvil
* In Teams rounds, armor that runs out of durability now stays on as a cosmetic without protection, so uniforms keep their look; turn this off with `/teams keepBrokenArmor false`
* Teams spectators can no longer hurt the players of a round, which they could while adventure mode was not forced, and a fighter who leaves a running round to spectate now dies like one changing sides
* Servers can now turn uncensored content on by default with `uncensoredContentByDefault`, leaving players free to turn it off, as an alternative to forcing it
