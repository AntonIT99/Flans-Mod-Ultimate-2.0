# Flan's Mod Ultimate 2.1

## Highlights

* Added a new HEAT / shaped-charge damage model
* Reworked blast and fragmentation explosions
* Reworked projectile gravity and drag, including realistic throw speeds
* Added throwable weapons such as javelins and pila
* Added a new dynamic aiming pose system
* Expanded shield mechanics with frontal melee/projectile blocking
* Added support for custom armor capes
* Added optional Distant Horizons integration

## Vehicles & Aircraft

* Reworked ground vehicle steering and added `RealTurnRateDegPerSec`
* Improved tracked vehicle turning and track animations
* Improved vehicle armor, hitboxes and hitbox HP balance
* Improved engine sounds and pitch behavior
* Improved aircraft collision boxes and helicopter stabilization
* Aircraft can remain active while passengers are aboard
* Passengers can take control when the pilot seat is empty
* Improved bomb release behavior
* Fixed various vehicle/projectile collision issues

## Weapons & Visuals

* Improved AA guns, deployed guns and vehicle weapon shooting points
* Added configurable AA gun traverse speed
* Added muzzle flashes and muzzle particles to more weapons and vehicles
* Added FMU-style and classic 1.12.2-style muzzle flash options
* Added `ForceAimPose`, `MirrorInLeftHand` and `ModeSwitchSound`
* Added toggling support for lightsabers, lasers and flashlights
* Improved explosion, fire, smoke and fragmentation effects

## Compatibility & API

* Expanded the Flan's Mod Ultimate API for addons and integrations
* Improved legacy content-pack compatibility
* Improved cross-version recipe and content handling
* Fixed Custom NPCs 64×32 texture rendering
* Fixed paintjobs with Minecraft's Pick Item function
* Fixed vehicle ammunition stacking
* Updated bundled vehicle armor, hitboxes, effects and content definitions

## Fixes

* Fixed mine explosions
* Fixed unregistered sounds
* Fixed vehicle muzzle positions and shooting points
* Fixed traverse sounds
* Fixed plane engine sounds
* Fixed steering-wheel rendering
* Fixed projectile penetration with unbreakable blocks
* Fixed several vehicle hitbox and collision issues
* Fixed the Sherman Calliope
* Fixed `OnWaterWalking`
* Various additional rendering, physics, networking, command and stability fixes
