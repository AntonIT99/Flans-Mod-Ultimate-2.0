# Attribution and Upstream Projects

Flan's Mod Ultimate 2 is an unofficial and independently maintained
modern continuation and reimplementation of the Flan's Mod ecosystem.

Its primary purpose is to provide a modern implementation of the legacy
Flan's Mod systems while maintaining compatibility with existing Flan's Mod
content packs wherever possible.

The project is substantially rewritten for modern versions of Minecraft and
Forge / NeoForge, but it originates from and remains technically compatible
with the legacy Flan's Mod family of projects described below.

## Upstream Lineage

The principal project lineage relevant to Flan's Mod Ultimate 2 is:

**Flan's Mod**  
→ **Flan's Mod Plus / Flan's Mod Ultimate**  
→ **Flan's Mod Ultimate Stability Edition**  
→ **Flan's Mod Ultimate 2**

### Flan's Mod

Flan's Mod was originally created by **Jamioflan** and subsequently developed
by numerous contributors.

Source:
https://github.com/FlansMods/FlansMod

Flan's Mod established the original gameplay systems, content-pack format,
and ecosystem on which the later Flan's Mod continuations were based.

The legacy source repository contains a Creative Commons
Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike license.

### Flan's Mod Plus / Flan's Mod Ultimate

Flan's Mod Ultimate was an unofficial continuation of Flan's Mod for
Minecraft 1.7.10.

Source:
https://github.com/war-monger/Flan-s-Mod-Plus-War_Monger

This project continued and expanded the original Flan's Mod implementation
and forms part of the upstream lineage of Flan's Mod Ultimate 2.

### Flan's Mod Ultimate Stability Edition

**Flan's Mod Ultimate Stability Edition (FMUSE)** is an unofficial continuation
of Flan's Mod Ultimate and is based on the Flan's Mod Plus / Flan's Mod Ultimate
codebase.

Source:
https://github.com/Unknown025/Flans-Mod-Plus

FMUSE was maintained primarily by **Unknown025** and **GoldSloth**, together
with other contributors.

This repository was the **primary legacy source and reference implementation**
used during the development of Flan's Mod Ultimate 2.

Flan's Mod Ultimate 2 therefore incorporates, ports, reimplements, or adapts
functionality originating from this codebase while substantially changing the
underlying implementation to support modern Minecraft versions.

The FMUSE repository includes the Creative Commons
Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike license used by the legacy Flan's Mod
codebase.

## Flan's Mod Ultimate 2

Flan's Mod Ultimate 2 is developed and maintained independently by **Wolff**.

Although it preserves compatibility with the legacy Flan's Mod ecosystem and
uses the projects above as its upstream lineage and reference implementations,
the modern project substantially changes and reimplements the original
architecture.

Major areas that have been modernized, rewritten, or newly implemented include:

- modern Forge / NeoForge registration and lifecycle integration
- modern networking and synchronization
- modern entity implementations
- rewritten rendering integration
- modern menus, screens, and user interfaces
- rewritten legacy content-pack loading and compatibility handling
- weapons, bullets, ammunition, grenades, and attachments
- deployable weapons
- vehicles and aircraft
- revised vehicle and aircraft physics
- modern configuration systems
- digital ammunition functionality
- content-pack packaging and management
- compatibility across multiple modern Minecraft versions

Compatibility with legacy content packs is intentionally retained where
possible, but the underlying implementation has been substantially modified
to operate with modern Minecraft, Forge, and NeoForge APIs.

Flan's Mod Ultimate 2 is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by
Jamioflan, the current Flan's Mod developers, the developers of Flan's Mod:
Reloaded, or the maintainers of the upstream projects listed above.
